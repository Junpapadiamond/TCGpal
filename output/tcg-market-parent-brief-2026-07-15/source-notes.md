# TCG market parent brief — source inventory

Reviewed 2026-07-15. The report distinguishes primary operating data, vendor research, market infrastructure documentation, and purposively selected community discussion. Community posts are qualitative signals only; they are not used to estimate population sentiment or investment returns.

## Community discussion sample

Eighteen public discussions from Reddit, X, Japanese boards, and Taiwan forums are coded in `community-sample.csv`. Selection was purposive: posts were chosen because they explicitly discussed boom mechanics, scarcity, affordability, reprints, nostalgia, play, grading, or exit liquidity. The resulting counts describe the sample only.

## Interpretation rules

1. Publisher and retailer growth does not prove that modern singles appreciate.
2. Asking-price charts are not the same as completed-sale indices.
3. Community volume is evidence of attention, not evidence of returns.
4. U.S.–Asia comparisons are directional because release calendars, languages, marketplaces, fees, and currency differ.
5. “Price steadiness” is treated partly as a measurement effect: U.S. reference tools often smooth multiple completed sales, while many Asian observations are local shop or spot asking prices.
