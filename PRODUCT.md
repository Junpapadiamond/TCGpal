# Product

## Register

product

## Users

U.S. raw-single trading-card buyers who already know the card they want and need help deciding whether a live listing is comparable, trustworthy, and worth opening. They may be checking Pokémon or One Piece today, with more TCGs planned.

## Product Purpose

TCGlens is an evidence-backed listing comparison tool. It confirms the exact card/version, fans out across configured concrete-listing sources, ranks condition-compatible listings by complete comparable cost, seller trust, and reviewable evidence, then returns one recommended buy with lens toggles for cheapest, safest, and best-documented alternatives. It should also abstain clearly when no listing is comparable.

Success means the buyer understands the recommendation, the sources, the assumptions, the missing information, and the confidence level without mistaking aggregate references or manual links for fetched seller inventory.

## Brand Personality

Careful, plainspoken, warm.

TCGlens should feel like a knowledgeable collecting friend who is allergic to fake certainty: calm enough to trust, detailed enough to audit, and light enough that checking a card does not feel like reading a tax filing.

## Anti-references

- Generic marketplace dashboards with dense tables, unexplained scores, and hidden source failures.
- Price-prediction or investment-advice apps that imply certainty beyond the evidence.
- Overanimated landing pages where motion competes with the buyer's decision.
- Messy wizard flows where later steps appear before the user has finished the current choice.
- AI-looking heroes with stacked badges, repeated uppercase eyebrows, decorative cards, and copy that sounds more poetic than useful.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the buyer's next decision. The primary screen should make it obvious what to do now.
2. Reveal complexity progressively. Start with the card, then confirm version, then compare listings.
3. Keep evidence legible. Every recommendation needs sources, timestamps, assumptions, missing data, and confidence.
4. Treat unknown as unknown. Missing seller or evidence fields should not become implied risk or implied safety.
5. Use delight to clarify state. Card motion should explain searching, fanning out, comparing, or selecting, not decorate every surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic headings, labels, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and usable desktop/mobile layouts. Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences. Copy and controls should work in English and 中文. Color contrast should meet WCAG AA, with special attention to muted text on the current cream/teal surface.
