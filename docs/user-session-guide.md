# TCGpal — 5-Session User Test Guide

**One question this test answers:** when a buyer is mid-decision on a *real* listing, does TCGpal's evidence actually change their next action? Behavior, not opinion. **North-star metric: decision-change rate.**

This is a lightweight 5-person probe, not the full 10-user × 30-listing pilot in `validation-plan.md`. Goal is one honest data point on the riskiest assumption.

## Setup (per session, ~20 min)

- **Recruit:** 5 people who bought a Pokémon raw single in the last 30 days.
- **Bring real stakes:** ask each to bring 2–3 listings they're *actually* weighing (eBay, TCGplayer, Facebook/Reddit, or a local shop). No hypotheticals.
- **Don't pitch first.** Watch the very first comparison cold. Record with consent or take notes.

## Protocol

1. **Baseline (2 min).** "Walk me through what you'd normally check before buying this card." Capture their manual workflow and roughly how long it takes.
2. **Cold run (silent).** Hand them TCGpal, ask them to look up the card / paste the listing. Say nothing. Start a timer. Log time-to-result, whether they corrected the confirmed version, and where they got stuck.
3. **Post-result questions** (from `validation-plan.md`):
   - Which part of this did you verify yourself?
   - **What, if anything, changed your next action?** ← the one that matters
   - Which lens was most useful: cost, seller safety, or condition evidence?
   - What evidence was still missing?
   - What would make you distrust this result?
4. **Repeat** the cold run for their 2nd/3rd listing.
5. **14-day follow-up.** One message: did they come back with a new listing on their own? Unprompted return is the strongest signal.

## What to log (drops straight into the tracker)

- **Per comparison:** source, completed Y/N, time-to-result, completed <90s, identity corrected Y/N, decision changed Y/Maybe/N, most-useful lens, missing evidence, demo/live.
- **Per user:** returned with a 2nd listing, shared a result or asked for saved history, baseline-workflow notes.

## Scaled gate (pass = keep investing)

- **≥3 of ~15** comparisons change the next action (decision-change rate ≈20%+).
- **≥10 of ~15** complete under 90 seconds.
- Identity correction **<10%**.
- **≥2 of 5** users return with another listing or ask for saved history.

## Read-outs / pivots (pre-committed in `validation-plan.md`)

- Evidence valued but ranking rejected → reposition as a neutral comparison ledger.
- They only want a price → stop adding agent complexity.
- Identity unreliable → fix catalog matching before any marketplace expansion.

## Do / don't

- **Do** record observed behavior, corrections, time, and exact quotes.
- **Don't** ask "is this useful?", explain features before the cold run, or lead the witness.
