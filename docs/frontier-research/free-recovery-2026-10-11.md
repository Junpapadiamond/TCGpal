## Decision challenge and smallest test

The underlying instinct is to preserve automatic cross-market coverage without additional cash cost. The proposed mechanism is to use existing monthly Apify credits for Whatnot and evaluate Crawlora's monthly free Mercari endpoint.

Strongest case: the existing Whatnot adapter has historical successful acquisitions and known price-unit mapping; Crawlora documents a much larger recurring free allowance than Soldgraph and an API suited to a server-side search. Serious objections: Crawlora's documented schema does **not** establish USD or active/unsold status, and neither this provider's terms nor its API existence establishes marketplace permission. Free allowances also remain finite, and historical Whatnot success does not prove the pinned build still works.

The founder subsequently requested investigation of self-hosting without third-party APIs and authorized free registration, answering the prepared Crawlora approval request. Crawlora Free was registered with basic Google profile consent; the research key stays in a git-ignored local secret file, with no Vercel credential or production integration. The bounded evaluation used two of the proposed 20 requests/60 free credits and stopped on missing required evidence. Separately requested: at most 100 additional Whatnot starts, $0.03 maximum per start drawn only from existing free credit, expiring 2026-11-01T00:00:00Z. That Apify extension is **not approved**. No card, paid upgrade or overage is authorized.

Evaluate five exact-card queries spanning Pokémon and One Piece, including Charizard ex 199/165, in two rounds. Start with one query and stop early when a kill criterion is demonstrated. Success requires useful repeatable matches, source-supported currency/activity and acquisition freshness, no exact-number/print conflicts in the admitted sample, bounded latency, and a successful deployed-environment test. Unknown charges remain null and cannot produce ranking winners. Kill on access denial/challenge, unsupported currency/activity/freshness, schema drift, hidden monetary expenditure, or unsupported identity/condition claims. No automatic retry via another transport after access denial.

Account creation alone does not promote the source. All five production promotion gates in AGENTS.md still apply. If the test cannot establish active USD asking prices, report the gap and keep Crawlora out of production.

## Self-hosting feasibility

The strongest case for self-hosting is control over request budgets and removal of a provider subscription dependency. Two serious objections remain: a new server does not grant marketplace access, and self-maintained parsing/reliability still has operational cost. Claims that free open-source code gives unlimited reliable inventory are unverified.

The smallest falsifiable test reused the existing transparent [Whatnot research collector](../../scripts/frontier-research/direct-metadata/whatnot-README.md), with the new founder request as its trigger. At **14:47:19.040–14:47:19.111 UTC**, its robots preflight returned **HTTP 403** in approximately 70 ms. It stopped after one robots request: zero searches, zero details and zero paid calls. `productionReady` remained false. Success would have required permitted access plus an exact raw USD listing with traceable current facts; the observed denial met the kill criterion. Owner: Codex; reviewed October 11. Do not follow the denial with fingerprint changes, proxies, sessions or private endpoint calls.

Read-only source inspection found:

- [omkarcloud/whatnot-scraper transport](https://github.com/omkarcloud/whatnot-scraper/blob/master/whatnot/fetch.py): MIT-licensed repository, but its transport impersonates browser TLS and supports a residential-exit fallback after 403/429. It uses reverse-engineered web GraphQL documents. No code was installed or executed. This implementation cannot simply be adopted within the current access boundary; removing those mechanisms would not prove access after the observed denial.
- [nicholasc861/Mercari-API handlers](https://github.com/nicholasc861/Mercari-API/blob/master/app/handler/handlers.go): a wrapper around HTML selectors, not an official inventory API. The item URL concatenation also omits the separator before the ID. It provides no evidence of current hosted acquisition reliability.
- [adamgauthier/mercari-client](https://github.com/adamgauthier/mercari-client): explicitly wraps a webpage parser. It does not solve upstream access or provide a demonstrated automatic search replacement.

The existing [Mercari direct evaluation](mercari-direct-2026-10-03.md) already demonstrated useful local DOM facts but not unattended hosted success. Its hosted 403 remains a separate unresolved constraint. No new server or public collector was created just to expose an unavailable source.
