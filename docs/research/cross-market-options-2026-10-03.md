## Decision

Prefer an official merchant feed that actually returns eligible US offers, then CardNexus and CardTrader for broader marketplace coverage. Do not renew a scraper allowance solely because its quota expired. The strongest case for additional spend is immediate concrete-listing coverage; the serious objections are paying for failed acquisition and mistaking a provider's marketing for permission, exact-print accuracy, or complete checkout cost.

Official feeds can still have stale inventory, incomplete shipping, buyer fees, and sparse US coverage. A configured adapter is not evidence of a working source. Unknown charges stay unknown; only independently supported complete costs may enter winner selection.

### CardNexus fee and identity caveats

The [July 27 terms](https://help.cardnexus.com/articles/5192860-terms-and-conditions) charge buyers 2.5% of the order amount plus 0.30 in the order currency; the order amount includes delivery/additional fees. Seller shipping is variable. Therefore a cheapest listing price alone cannot be declared a complete pre-tax total. The platform license also warrants review for third-party public display.

Its [pricing snapshot](https://docs.cardnexus.com/pricing/snapshot) returns regional aggregates: North America uses USD, but values can be converted from a seller's original currency and include graded listings in top-level totals. These aggregate floors must not become concrete raw-USD candidates. The [catalog feed](https://docs.cardnexus.com/feeds/catalog) carries external IDs, but crosswalks still require exact-print verification.

## Options that do not solve today's Pokémon/One Piece comparison

- **TCGplayer directly:** [current getting-started guide](https://docs.tcgplayer.com/docs/getting-started) still says no new API access. Existing TCGCSV aggregate references do not expose seller offers. Affiliate membership is not listing API access.
- **Cardmarket directly:** [official access page](https://help.cardmarket.com/en/cardmarket-api) still rejects new applications. US delivery and USD checkout eligibility were not established by this investigation; do not invent them from European prices.
- **Mana Pool:** [official seller page](https://manapool.com/seller-info) describes an API-supported marketplace built for Magic. Useful for a future MTG adapter, not the current two games.
- **JustTCG:** [features](https://justtcg.com/features) describe condition/printing pricing blended from marketplace data and reported store sales. This is reference pricing, not a documented concrete-offer replacement.
- **Troll and Toad:** [current homepage](https://www.trollandtoad.com/) redirects to a maintenance/password page and says orders are paused during inventory relocation. Buylist remains open; that is not buyer inventory.
- **COMC:** public homepage fetch returned HTTP 403 in this review. No documented public buyer-listing API or feed grant was found. Stop at the restriction; absence of a search result does not prove no partnership exists.
- **CoolStuffInc and merchant partnerships:** [homepage](https://www.coolstuffinc.com/) lists both games, but no authorized buyer-listing API/feed was established. Merchant-provided CSV/JSON/Shopify access could be a useful bounded source after an actual agreement and sample, not a speculative live badge.
