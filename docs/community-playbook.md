# Community answering playbook — help first, never spam

Goal: become a genuinely useful regular in TCG communities. The tool is a footnote you earn the right to mention, never the pitch.

## Rules of the game
1. **Answer the actual question fully — before (or instead of) mentioning anything.** The analysis is the value; the tool is optional.
2. **Be a real collector, not a brand.** Use your personal account with real history. Never a fresh "TCGpal official" account dropping links.
3. **10:1 rule.** Give ~10 helpful answers for every 1 time you mention the tool.
4. **Let curiosity pull, don't push.** The best moment to mention it is when someone asks "how did you check that so fast?" — pulled, not pushed.
5. **Disclose when you mention it.** "Full disclosure, I built a little tool for this." Honesty matches the product brand and disarms the shill reflex.
6. **Read each community's rules.** Many subs ban all self-promo. There you NEVER link — you just help, and let your profile do the work.
7. **No copy-paste.** Don't post the same reply across threads. Mods and users smell it instantly.
8. **Avoid:** brand voice, urgency, "check out my app!", emoji spam, links in cold replies.

## Where to be (and the norm)
- **Reddit** — r/PokemonTCG, r/PokeInvesting, r/pkmntcgtrades, r/tcgcollecting. Strict self-promo rules; default to no links, help only, link in profile.
- **Discord** — trading/grading/set-specific servers. Ask mods before sharing anything; most have a #self-promo or #tools channel.
- **CN** — 小红书 (content-led, post helpful breakdowns), 集换社 微信群, 闲鱼 评论区. Lead with content, never cold links.

I'd ask the seller for a back photo + confirm the set number before offering. Not a definite no, just worth a 30-second check.

(No tool mention needed. The value stands alone.)

### EN — "Is this real / legit?"
> Couple of checks: the cert number should verify on PSA's site (free) — if it doesn't, walk away. Also confirm it's the [exact version, e.g. 125/094 SIR] and not a reprint/proxy; the easy tell is [detail]. Photos of the back + corners would help rule out condition surprises.

### EN — "Should I buy now or wait? (FOMO)"
> Honest framework I use: is the price inside what you'd planned to spend, is the version 100% confirmed, and have you seen recent *sold* comps (not just the asking)? If any of those is a no, that's usually the "wait" signal, not the price going up. The "last one ever" feeling is almost always wrong.

## Cadence
- Block ~30 min/day in ONE channel first. Help, don't sell.
- Track which answers get upvotes/thanks — those topics are your aha signal.
- After 2 weeks, you'll know: do people want this answer, and do they ask "how"? That pull rate is your earliest demand signal.

## The meta point
How you behave in communities (honest, helpful, no hype) must be identical to what the product is. Consistency between founder and product = trust. That trust is the distribution.
