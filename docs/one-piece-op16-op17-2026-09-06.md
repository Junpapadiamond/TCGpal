# OP16 / OP17 coverage and independent artwork results

The catalog includes all 155 official OP16 release entries and all 169 official OP17
release entries. Cards sharing a collector number retain separate print IDs, official
images, selection, comparison caches and market-reference resolution. A distinct artwork
does not guarantee available inventory or enough listing evidence for a buy recommendation.

## Scope and source evidence

Observed 2026-09-06:

- [Official English OP16 list](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/cardlist/?series=569116):
  all 155 print IDs already existed, including six inserts with older collector numbers.
- [Official English OP17 list](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/cardlist/?series=569117):
  169 entries, including 158 OP17-number prints and 11 older-number inserts.
- `one-piece-card-game-json@0.2.55` cross-check: every official OP16/OP17 print ID, name,
  rarity and card type matched the structured dataset. Each artwork uses the corresponding
  official image, without inferring Manga/SP identity from an ordinal suffix.
- OP17 includes ST31-004_p1 and ST32-002_p1. Their two base siblings were included as well
  to avoid false single-print uniqueness. The base names were checked against the official
  [ST31](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/cardlist/?series=569031) and
  [ST32](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/cardlist/?series=569032) lists.

The scoped supplement in `scripts/lib/one-piece-op17-source.json` contains only the
169 OP17 entries and those two base siblings. `node scripts/build-optcg-catalog.mjs`
reproduces the combined snapshot. All 4,571 pre-existing catalog rows are unchanged.
The rest of the newer package's starter decks and events were not imported by this update.
The current snapshot has 4,742 prints / 2,755 families; ST31/ST32 coverage is partial.
The [official print-ID fixture](../src/lib/testing/one-piece-op16-op17-official.json)
records URLs, observation times and source-page hashes. Raw page archives are not committed.

## Behavior and safety boundary

The buyer's intent is to compare the artwork they selected. The strongest reason for
separate results is that same-number prints can differ radically in price and evidence.
Two constraints prevent forcing a different winner: some artworks have no eligible live
listing, and two parallel artworks may share every seller-visible label. The chosen behavior
preserves independent identity and abstains when evidence cannot separate siblings.

The existing classifier engine, retail-only base silence rule, alt-beside-SP guard and
human mapping-approval boundary remain intact. No new curated product IDs or suffix-derived
Manga claims were added. In particular, OP17-079_p1 and OP17-079_p2 remain separate picker
choices but currently lack enough unique text proof to assign separate market products.
This change cannot detect a seller whose text names the right print while the photo shows
another artwork; the image-proof decision remains separate.

Fixes:

- The live catalog fallback merged by collector number and dropped a real Ace artwork.
  It now merges by print ID; a failing test reproduced the loss before the fix.
- Release filtering recognizes OP16/OP17 names independently of a card's original number.
  “Luffy OP17” includes EB04-061_p2 instead of silently filtering it out.
- Identity and report caches include the catalog revision, preventing results based on
  an older sibling census from surviving the update.
- Current census expectations and the image-research cohort guard follow the updated
  catalog. The earlier 629-image experiment remains historical; no new image comparison
  was claimed for the 642-print text-twin cohort.

added sibling substitutions or self-rejections. Kill criterion: any missing official print,
cross-artwork cache reuse, or admitted sibling in the invariant audit.
